Contour Study

Documented the decaying geometries of Bremen's WWII-era submarine docks, extracting their inherent structural language as conceptual material. Through photography investigation of these militaristic ruins, isolated compelling formal elements that embody forced perspective and contained absence. Translated these archaeological observations into a digital artifact—a minimalist structure suspended between water and sky—distilling the concrete fortification's imposing presence into abstracted form. The resulting 30-second animation recontextualizes historical military infrastructure as floating speculative architecture, transforming oppressive confined space into contemplative void. This visual meditation explores how sites of historical trauma can be processed through digital reinterpretation.
