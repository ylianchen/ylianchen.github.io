The Window

When the content of the media does not serve the public, people cannot obtain effective information from it. Using Construct2, I made a game about discovering the truth. Players operate a floating window that can see-through everything underneath, players need to avoid anti-virus program that appear on the webpage. At the same time look for items to make the window larger enable to see more information underneath.
